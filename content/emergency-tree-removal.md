# Emergency Tree Removal Knoxville — 24/7 Storm Response

When a tree falls on your house, blocks your driveway, or is hanging dangerously after a storm, you don't need to wait. Tree Removal Knoxville provides emergency response around the clock.

## Emergency Situations We Handle

- Trees on homes, garages, or structures
- Fallen trees blocking roads or driveways
- Trees on power lines (we don't touch lines — we coordinate with the utility)
- Partially broken trees at risk of further collapse
- Trees that have uprooted and damaged landscaping or fencing
